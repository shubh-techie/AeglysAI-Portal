# Speaker invitation template

Template only. Replace bracketed fields and confirm proposed arrangements before sending.
Keep recipient contacts, responses and internal correspondence outside this public repository.

**Subject:** Invitation to speak — AeglysAI [Challenge name]

Dear [Speaker name],

I would like to invite you to contribute a guest session to [event name] in the
AeglysAI AI/ML and Cybersecurity Hackathon program. AeglysAI explores adaptive authorization,
behavioral risk, cloud-native security and resilient distributed systems through
open-source engineering and research: https://github.com/shubh-techie/AeglysAI.

We propose a session on [topic and scope], aimed at platform engineers, cloud engineers,
security engineers, SREs, software engineers, AI/ML engineers, researchers and graduate
students. The planned event launch/submission window is [months from event configuration];
the session date and time zone are [pending or agreed date/time zone].

The proposed format is [technical talk / demonstration / discussion; remote or in person],
with an expected duration of [presentation minutes] plus [Q&A minutes]. Format, accessibility
arrangements and duration will be agreed with you before confirmation. Recording or
redistribution is not assumed and requires separate permission.

Please accept or decline through [organizer's private correspondence channel] by
[response deadline]. If interested, share your preferred topic, availability, session
outline and any logistical needs.

Please also supply a short bio, approved professional title/organization, topics,
optional LinkedIn URL and a photo you are authorized to share. Explicitly indicate
whether we may display each supplied item on the public AeglysAI community page. We will
publish only the approved profile after participation is confirmed; an invitation is not
confirmation. You may decline public-profile display independently of the speaking invitation.
Do not include private contact details in your public bio.

Thank you for considering sharing your experience with the community.

Shubh Prabhat
Creator & Maintainer, AeglysAI
