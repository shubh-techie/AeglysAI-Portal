# Two-round judging framework

COM-003B-FIX-001 finalized 2026-10-11. Weights match the locally inspected research community/hackathon/JUDGING.md; source approval/publication remains separate. Portal source of truth: src/data/hackathons.ts. No judges, finalists or results are claimed.

## Round 1 — technical screening

Review source code, executable prototype, required maximum-two-minute video and reproducibility. Select finalists, not winners.

| Category | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Functionality | 30 |
| AI/ML or intelligent feature | 25 |
| Relevance and creativity | 20 |
| Reproducibility | 15 |
| Video clarity | 10 |

## Round 2 — finalist ranking

Five-minute live demonstration over Google Meet, then five minutes of technical Q&A. Round 2 determines final ranking; do not combine round scores.

| Category | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Live demonstration | 35 |
| Technical understanding | 25 |
| Solution quality and safety | 20 |
| Technical Q&A | 15 |
| Presentation | 5 |

Propose five to ten finalist teams subject to submission quality and organizer capacity; publish capacity before opening, not a guaranteed count. Each rubric totals 100 points. Equally weighted non-conflicted judges score independently; average scores within each round. Qualification is eligibility, separate from scoring.

## Ties, conflicts and clarification

Round 1 ties: functionality, intelligent feature, then reproducibility. Round 2 ties: live demonstration, technical understanding, then solution quality and safety. Remaining ties receive equal additional demonstration/Q&A opportunity and independent panel review; if still equal, record a shared rank and explain capacity decisions under published rules. No hidden tie-break after reviewing scores.

Judges disclose membership, personal/financial relationships, supervision and mentoring conflicts privately. Recuse and assign unconflicted replacements for equivalent review. Keep assignments, scores, reasons and corrections in private Sheets/Drive. Publish only approved results and consented identities.

Technical clarification uses equivalent time/scope for similarly situated teams and is logged privately. No undisclosed post-deadline feature development. Apply published correction/disqualification procedures consistently, with documented responses before final decisions. Deadlines, decision authority and appeal channel must be configured before operation.

Distinguish original work from reused libraries/data/models; require attribution, licensing and disclosed AI-assistance scope/human verification. Do not upload confidential material to assistants. Teams explain their executable intelligent behavior and remain responsible for safety/correctness.
