The organizer’s follow-up explicitly authorizes adding all seven public onboarding links. All seven open flags are now true following the organizer’s explicit follow-up to add the links. Inactive UI provides contextual status, not a broken or disabled registration button. For subsequent opening changes, retain separate approval and recheck URL, signed-out access, policy, permissions and deadlines. Mark available=false whenever closed or inaccessible, independently of open. Never expose administrative URLs, spreadsheet IDs, Drive folders, responses or credentials. The website performs no browser Sheets calls and persists no participant data.

FORM-02 is team formation, not qualification. FORM-04 is profile/acceptance for invited judges, not evidence of an appointment or a general judging application. FORM-03 combines speakers and mentors. ## Fields actually observed and organizer actions

- FORM-01: name, contact email, GitHub identity, institution/status, preferred challenge, participation preference, rules and privacy acknowledgments. Confirm why institution/status/contact are necessary and ensure acknowledgments link to actual finalized notices, retention and eligibility terms.
- FORM-02: team name, leader contact/registration/GitHub identifiers, member 2–4 registration identifiers, challenge and roster agreement. Privately enforce maximum four, individually selected members, unique team ID, no duplicate membership and approved changes. Website cannot enforce roster eligibility.
- FORM-03: name, role/organization, contact, session title/abstract/format and headshot link. Add/confirm separate public-profile consent and approved biography/LinkedIn collection if needed; an image link alone grants no display rights.
- FORM-04: professional name/title/organization/contact, LinkedIn, biography, tracks, headshot and conflict declaration. Confirm invitation/appointment separately and obtain explicit public-profile/photo consent.
- FORM-05: name/title/organization/contact, capacity, headshot and public-profile consent. Confirm actual guest role and collect an approved short biography/LinkedIn privately if needed. Response does not establish attendance.
- FORM-06: team identity/contact, challenge, project title/description, source repository, README/setup, maximum-two-minute video, intelligent-feature explanation and originality confirmation. No separate test/validation, attribution/licensing, known-limitations or public-project consent field was observed. Organizers must add them or clearly require them through README sections and review criteria; permission/declarations must be explicit. Optional deployed demo may be added. Do not authorize showcase publication merely from submission.
- FORM-07: registration/contact/GitHub identity, fork/qualification branch/document/sanitized logs, architecture, observation/improvement and secrets declaration. Ensure setup/run and actual validation evidence are required in the qualification document. A/B/C sufficient; Model D not required; review/selection remains separate.
